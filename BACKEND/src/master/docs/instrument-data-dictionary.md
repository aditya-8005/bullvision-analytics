# Discovery Notes

## Cash Equity Representation

Based on exploration of the Angel One Instrument Master:

### NSE Cash Equity

Characteristics:

- instrumenttype = ""
- exch_seg = "NSE"
- symbol ends with "-EQ"

Example:

symbol: RELIANCE-EQ

---

### BSE Cash Equity

Characteristics:

- instrumenttype = ""
- exch_seg = "BSE"

Example:

symbol: RELIANCE

---

Derivatives use dedicated instrument types such as:

- FUTSTK
- OPTSTK

and belong to the NFO exchange segment.
